<assumptions_and_boundaries>
Import sampling policy under agent discretion: choose the nearest supported longest-edge editing count among 1024, 2048, 3072, 4096; ties round upward and sources beyond 4096 cap at 4096. Preserve intrinsic source dimensions separately. For generated maps, accept positive initial grid counts and one supported editing count through a domain creation contract; the Grid UI remains Phase 4. Empty/zero counts or source dimensions are invalid inputs, not a zero-size map. Preserve Background then Foreground ordering and source-raster order. Adjacent grid cells share one geometry transform and cannot accumulate a seam or change map extent when only counts differ.
</assumptions_and_boundaries>

</prohibitions>
