Placement and transform P2-05
Stamp tool · S
Placing — the ghost is the result before the click

Each click commits one stamp and placement stays active, so a row of trees is a row of clicks. Esc or another tool leaves placement; leaving does not undo what you already placed. The ghost carries every current placement setting, including the shadow, so what you see is what lands.

Selected — transform handles
Dragging a handle

A corner scales on both axes and keeps the aspect ratio by default; an edge handle scales one axis. Shift inverts the lock so a corner can go nonuniform. Nonuniform scale is allowed while editing and refused at export preflight, which names the offending stamps.

The drag is a preview. Release commits one undoable command; Esc mid-drag cancels with nothing committed. Proposed: Alt scales about the anchor instead of the opposite corner.

Rotating

Degrees, positive clockwise, wrapping at 360 (370 → 10). One stamp rotates about its anchor, not its box centre, so a tree pivots at its trunk. Proposed: Shift snaps to 15°.

An asset declaring a rotation limit clamps to it and the readout says so rather than silently ignoring the drag. A rotation-locked asset shows no rotation handle.

Browser gestures, and the way round dragging
click

Selects the asset in the browser. Shows its defaults. Places nothing.

double-click

Selects it and arms the Stamp tool, so the next canvas click places. The same as click then S.

drag to canvas

Places one stamp where you release and stays in placement. A convenience, never the only route.

keyboard only

Arrow keys move the grid focus, Enter arms placement, F6 reaches the canvas, Enter places at the cursor. Nothing requires a drag.

What the next click will place. Carried across asset changes — choose another pine and the size, rotation, tint and opacity stay. Changing anything here never touches a stamp already on the map.

Numeric entry, everywhere

Arrow keys step 1, Shift 10, Alt 0.1 where decimals exist. Enter commits and clamps, Esc restores the last committed value, Tab commits and moves. The label scrubs on drag. Out of range turns the field red and commits nothing. Ctrl+Z inside a focused field undoes text, not the document — stated in the panel, as in Phase 1.
