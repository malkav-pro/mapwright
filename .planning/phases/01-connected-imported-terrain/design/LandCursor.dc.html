Foreground
Three things, three places
Coverage

Only ever under the cursor, tinting the footprint so you can see placement and size before you commit. It decides land, sea and where the coast runs.

Texture colour

The pigment the texture brush deposits. Never confusable with the other two — it is the map’s own colour.

Layer opacity

Only ever on the layer row, as a bar with a number. Fades the whole of Foreground and leaves the coastline exactly where it was.

Coverage and opacity both look like “less of this layer”, so they never share a surface: coverage appears under the cursor and nowhere else, opacity appears in the layers panel and nowhere else. Neither gets a swatch, a preview or a legend competing with the map.

The tint is a cursor decoration, not a document view: it disappears with the cursor, never appears in an export, and costs nothing to draw.
