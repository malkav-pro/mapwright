Preview, commit, save — the three words this design keeps apart
Preview

What a drag shows before release. Discardable by Esc, never in history, never in an export.

Commit

One gesture becomes one undoable command on pointer release, Enter or blur. It advances the revision and appears in History.

Save

The commit is on disk and acknowledged. Only then does the chip read Saved; a visible canvas proves nothing about durability.
