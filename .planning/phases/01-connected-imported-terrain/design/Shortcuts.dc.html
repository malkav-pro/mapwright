Shortcuts by scope, and numeric entry

A key belongs to exactly one scope. The scope is whatever has keyboard focus — nothing is global except the four in the last block.

Out-of-range input: the field turns red and stays focused, nothing is committed, Enter clamps to the nearest bound and Esc restores. Non-numeric text is refused on commit with the same wording. Ranges are design proposals for engineering to confirm; no renderer maximum is invented here.
